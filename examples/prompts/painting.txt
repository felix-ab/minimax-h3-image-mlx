The image is a traditional Chinese gongbi painting on aged silk in warm ivory tones. A pair of red-crowned cranes stands among tall reeds at the edge of a pond: one crane lowers its head toward the water while the other raises its neck, beak slightly open. Every feather is drawn with fine, even ink lines and filled with delicate layered washes of white, grey and black, and the crowns are touched with vermilion. Lotus leaves in muted green and blue float on the pond, and a single pink lotus flower is half open. In the upper right corner, a vertical column of four elegant black calligraphy characters reads "松鹤延年", followed by a small square red seal. The silk shows faint craquelure and soft discolouration at the edges.
